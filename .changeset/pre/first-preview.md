First preview. TSX email templates with typed data and messages, compiled to HTML, plain text and a
dependency-free module; `email-kit init`, `new`, `compile`, `check` and `tokens`; themes from shadcn
CSS or DTCG tokens with dark mode; fourteen components; ICU plurals, locale fallback and
pseudo-locales; 100 of the planned 175 checks. Not yet: a page layout and footers, testing in real
email clients, and the preview studio.
